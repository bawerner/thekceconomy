Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW), covered employment under state unemployment insurance programs. "Jobs" is the average of the quarter's three monthly employment counts. Total wages are annualized by multiplying the quarter's wages by four; average weekly wage is based on the quarter's wages and jobs.

To protect employer confidentiality, BLS withholds some figures. Where a county's figure was withheld, the metro value is shown as "at least" (≥) and the gap appears as "Not disclosed". Small categories are grouped into "Other" to keep the chart readable.
